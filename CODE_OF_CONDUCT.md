# Code of conduct

AWEN contributors must provide a respectful, harassment-free environment in
repository discussions, reviews, issues, and project events.

Acceptable conduct includes constructive technical disagreement, specific and
actionable feedback, respect for privacy and attribution, and prompt correction
of mistakes. Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory
or sexualized language, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption,
or publishing another person's private information without permission.

The maintainer may edit or remove abusive content, warn participants, impose a
temporary participation restriction, or permanently ban a participant. Actions
will be proportional to severity, pattern, and risk. Retaliation against a
reporter is prohibited.

Report conduct incidents privately through
https://github.com/marcpoliquin5/awen/security/advisories/new and identify the
report as a code-of-conduct matter. Include links or screenshots and any safety
concerns. The maintainer will protect confidentiality to the extent practical,
acknowledge the report within three business days, and disclose conflicts of
interest before handling it.
