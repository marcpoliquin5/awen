## Results

- Measured by us: at the registered shapes (1-D 256 at 4 levels, 512 at 5; 2-D 28x28 at 2) and small cases, forward
  and inverse transforms match the reference within 1e-12 (a test tolerance, not a result), energy is preserved,
  gradients equal the reference transpose and `gradcheck` passes in 2-D, band labels match, and a Haar block with
  identity band mixing reconstructs its input.
- So Haar's MNIST accuracy (0.4126 vs dense 0.9827, stored result on #161) and Burgers gap are not explained by a
  wrong transform, inverse, gradient or band layout.
- Not tested, hypothesis only: the registered layer has one `width x width` mixing matrix per band (3 on MNIST),
  shared by every position in that band, so it has no position-dependent weights. A changed layer would be a new,
  separately registered variant.
