## Results

Source: measured by us, `awen-models/results/photon-recalibration/results.json` (L1). Model B at its sourced primary
point (ENOB 7.61 and 8-bit weights, hua2025 measured; 30 dB extinction ratio, wang2018 measured). Seeds 1-3, 10,000
MNIST test images, registered rule (paired drop from no drift, 95% t upper bound at most 0.01).

| Regime | Tolerable drift budget (rms per interval, normalized weight units) |
| --- | --- |
| standard | 0.05 |
| noise-aware-100 | 0.1 |
| noise-aware-1000 | 0.05 |

Exploratory: noise-aware-100 is flatter but less accurate than standard at every budget from 0 to 0.1 (reverses);
noise-aware-1000 reverses at 0 to 0.02 and shows no detectable difference from 0.05 up. Budgets are free parameters
(no sourced drift rate), so no recalibration time in seconds or hours follows. The recomputed no-drift point matches
E-022 for 8 of 9 runs; standard seed 1 differs by 3 of 10,000 images (stated in the results README).
