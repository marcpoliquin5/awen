| Contract | Lowered / fallback nodes | Lowered MACs | logits max abs diff | rel. L2 | top-1 agreement |

| ideal | 21 / 107 | 47,585,280 | 1.43e-05 | 4.4e-07 | 1.0000 |

The 21 lowered nodes are the 20 `AwenLinear` layers plus the tied output head. Host fallbacks: 5 sdpa, 11 layer_norm,
5 gelu, 2 embedding, the rest layout, indexing and adds. Conversions: 221,184 input DAC, 991,360 weight DAC and
743,520 ADC. These numbers are not comparable one-to-one with E-014 (the Runtime path keeps the head on the host), and
torch-backend v1 reuses one seed for every lowered GEMM of a call. No device, energy, latency or speedup claim.
