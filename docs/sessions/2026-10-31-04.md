## Results

Counted by the report code from FX shapes at `a62abd9`, stored in all three records: 47,585,280 lowered MACs,
2,949,120 host MACs (5 attention cores x 4 heads x 48 x 48 positions x (32 + 32)), no uncounted node, lowered MAC
fraction 0.94164. This is a dense count; with causal masking subtracted the host share would be smaller. The host
count equals the independent Runtime-path count in `awen.capability` (P1-5) minus its unrouted tied head (test
`test_host_fallback_macs_match_the_runtime_path_count`).

Measured by us (simulator output): the logit deviations equal the E-018 records of the same contract and seed policy
(default per_gemm rel. L2 0.7409, top-1 0.0833; shared 0.8462 / 0.0208; ideal 4.4e-07 / 1.0), and `lowered`,
`conversions` and `backend` of `run-a62abd9.json` equal `run-a6db2f4.json`. One seed and one input, so no confidence
interval. MAC counts are not time, energy or speedup. Recorded as E-019.
