## Results

No experiment was run. The new numbers are peer-reviewed and measured (demarinis2021, Section 4.1 and Table 1, one
silicon-on-insulator device):

- **Average per-MZI extinction ratio:** 26.8 dB near 1550 nm (sigma 4.2 dB).
- **Worst MZI:** 22.9 dB (MZI 9, bar state, 1550 nm).
- **Best MZI:** 42.5 dB (MZI 6, cross state, 1550 nm).

These are per-element contrasts. They are not the contract's tile-level crosstalk and not a system figure.
