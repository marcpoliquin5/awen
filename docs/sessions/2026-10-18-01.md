## Results (pilots; not evidence for the hypotheses)

All measured by us on the CPU above, lossless-unitary or transfer-matrix simulation (L0/L1),
one seed unless stated.

- H1 pilot, N = 8, 16 Haar targets, fidelity F = |Tr(V†U)|²/N²: at depth 6 a butterfly mesh
  (24 MZIs) reached mean F 0.979 vs 0.810 for Clements at depth 6 (21 MZIs); Clements reached
  F ≥ 0.999 for all targets at depth 8 (28 MZIs), butterfly at depth 9 (36 MZIs) and needs
  waveguide crossings (18 per worst path in our layout model). N = 16: butterfly F ≥ 0.999 at
  depth 16 (128 MZIs).
- H3 pilot: Fibonacci trace-map invariant constant to 1.2e-8 where the traces stay bounded;
  quarter-wave transmission repeats with period 6 in generation (Kohmoto et al. 1987). Cantor
  stacks: the best spectral self-similarity factor between generations was 2.3-2.5, not the
  naive factor 3 (negative result for that prediction).
- H2 pilot (Monarch FFN, 4x wider hidden at matched parameters, 1500 steps): val loss 1.765 and
  1.740 (seeds 1, 2) vs dense baseline 1.721 and 1.715; batch-1 latency about 2.8x higher.
  Negative so far; Kronecker and recursive variants still running.
