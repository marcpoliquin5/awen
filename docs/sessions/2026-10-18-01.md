## Results (pilots; not evidence for the hypotheses)

All measured by us on the CPU above (L0/L1), one seed unless stated, stored at commit `2897471`
under `research/experiments/multiplexing-fractal/results/`.

- H1 pilot (`meshes_n8.json`), N = 8, 16 Haar targets, fidelity F = |Tr(V†U)|²/N²: at depth 6 a
  butterfly mesh (24 MZIs) reached mean F 0.979 vs 0.810 for Clements at depth 6 (21 MZIs).
  Clements reached F ≥ 0.999 on all targets at depth 8 (28 MZIs); butterfly at depth 9 (36 MZIs),
  with 18 waveguide crossings on the worst path in our layout model.
- H1 pilot (`meshes_n16.json`), N = 16, 8 targets: butterfly mean F 0.9999 (min 0.99974) at depth 16
  (128 MZIs).
- H3 pilot (`stacks.json`): Fibonacci trace-map invariant constant to 1.2e-8 where the traces stay
  bounded; quarter-wave transmission repeats with period 6 in generation (Kohmoto et al. 1987).
  Cantor stacks: the best spectral self-similarity factor between generations was 2.3-2.5, not the
  naive factor 3 (negative result for that prediction).
- H2 pilot (`h2_pilot_partial_runs.jsonl`, seeds 1 and 2, 1500 steps): Monarch FFN with 4x wider
  hidden at matched parameters, val loss 1.765 and 1.740 vs dense baseline 1.721 and 1.715.
  Batch-1 latency 3.59 and 3.52 ms vs 1.25 and 1.46 ms, measured while two training processes
  shared the CPU. Negative so far.
- H2 Kronecker (`kron3`), `recursive` and `recursive-monarch` variants: not run to completion in
  this session (training was in progress). Command:
  `python research/experiments/multiplexing-fractal/h2_structured_weights.py --seeds 1 2 3`.
