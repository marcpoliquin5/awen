## Results

Counts are measured by us with the `--check` command above (source: measured, stored output in the PR body).

- 95 hard-coded device literals in `awen-compiler`: 76 have no checked value in the assumption set yet
  (unsourced), 15 are definitional (ideal noise contract, identity calibration, zero-initialized
  accumulators),
  1 matches it (noise contract `dac_bits` 8 = PACE weight DAC, Hua et al. 2025), and 3 differ:
  the `reference-pace-like-128` descriptor declares `dac_bits` 10 (PACE: 8), `effective_bits` 8
  (PACE system ENOB: 7.61) and `adc_energy_pj_per_sample` 4.0 pJ, which is below both published ADC
  designs in the file (6.35 pJ at 10.0 ENOB, 0.5 GS/s; 31.7 pJ at 9.4 ENOB, 5 GS/s).
- 10 of the 95 describe digital CPU/GPU baselines (energies and precisions); the v0 assumption set has
  no digital-baseline section.
- 22 belong to the reference calibration fixture `reference-room-temperature-v1`, which carries a
  `measured_at` timestamp although no device was measured (nothing in AWEN is L3); labeled as synthetic
  in the inventory, not changed.
