## Results

No experiment was run. The new numbers are peer-reviewed and measured (vaskasi2022, Section 3.1, one laser):

- **RIN:** below -150 dB/Hz up to 6 GHz at 50 and 60 mA, 20 °C. This is an upper bound, not a level. The noise contract
  applies RIN flat over the detection bandwidth (10 GHz built-in), so using the bound above 6 GHz is an extrapolation.
- **Intrinsic linewidth at 60 mA:** 440 kHz at 20 °C and 28 kHz at 22 °C (after tuning the external-feedback phase).

These are laser figures for one device. They are not a system figure.
